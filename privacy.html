Privacy Policy

Ratan Bhaban Rent Management System

Last updated: November 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Ratan Bhaban Rent Management System handles information when you use the application.

1. Information We Handle

The application may handle information entered by its users, including:

  • Tenant names and rental information.
  • Rent payments and outstanding balances.
  • Expense and financial transaction records.
  • Loan and large expense records.
  • Other information voluntarily entered by the user.

2. Data Storage

The application is designed to store records in the browser's local storage on the device where the application is used. Data may be lost if browser storage is cleared or the device is changed. Users should maintain regular backups.

3. Backup and Google Drive

The application may provide optional Google Drive backup and restore functionality. If you choose to connect Google Drive, the application may access the Google Drive permissions requested during authorization to perform the selected backup or restore operations.

Backup files stored in Google Drive are subject to Google's privacy and security practices. You can manage or revoke the application's access through your Google Account security settings.

4. Information Sharing

We do not intentionally sell or rent users' financial or tenant information to third parties. Information may be shared with Google when you choose to use Google Drive integration.

5. Data Security

Users are responsible for protecting their devices, browser profiles, backup files, and Google Accounts. No electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Records stored in browser storage remain there until deleted by the user or removed through browser or device actions. Backup copies in Google Drive must be managed or deleted from the user's Google Drive account.

7. Children's Privacy

This application is intended for property and rent management purposes and is not designed for children.

8. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised date.
